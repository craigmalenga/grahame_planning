Paste any of the prompts below into **ChatGPT (with image generation enabled)** or
**Midjourney**. Each prompt is self-contained and references our exact measured
dimensions from `drawings/dimensions.yaml` (Rev B).

⚠️ **Important:** generative-AI images cannot be relied on for dimensional
accuracy — every render will be a stylistic interpretation, not a measured
drawing. Use them as **board / family presentation visuals** alongside the
2D planning drawings in the pack. The 2D drawings remain the source of
dimensional truth.

For best fidelity, generate **multiple times** and pick the closest. You can
also paste a screenshot of `drawings/output/png/10_courtyard_3d.png` (my

will use it as a layout/composition guide.

## Style modifiers (append to any prompt for variation)

- `, in the style of an architects' presentation render, Vray-quality`
- `, by Hayes Davidson` (for marketing-style)
- `, sympathetic conservation-area aesthetic, restrained, no glossy CGI gimmicks`
- `, day-grey overcast, no harsh shadows`
- `, golden-hour daylight just after sunrise` (for warmer mood)
- `, no figures, no plants, just architecture`

## What to AVOID prompting

- "Modern", "minimalist" — this is a Victorian listed building, period detail must be respected
- Bright primary colours — palette is restrained (yellow brick, red brick, white paint, black iron, cream plaster)
- Wide-angle perspective distortion — 35–50 mm equivalent focal length feels right for architectural renders
- People — they distract from the architecture
- Furniture in the courtyard — the brief is to keep it open

## Iteration workflow

1. Generate each prompt 4× and pick the closest match to my axonometric.
2. If a render gets the L-shape wrong (e.g. flat wall, no recess) — add to the
   prompt: *"the dwelling's two rear walls form an INSIDE CORNER (L-shape) —
   the rear wall is shorter and the side wall is longer, meeting at right angles."*
3. If the staircase ends up centred rather than tucked into the corner — add:
   *"the staircase sits TIGHT to the inside corner of the L — its outer tread
   envelope kisses both walls simultaneously."*
4. If the bricked-up opening is rendered as a window — add: *"the bricked-up
   opening on the side wall is currently BLANK BRICKWORK with a faint outline
   of the former opening; it is NOT a window in the existing condition."*

Save the best render of each view at high resolution (≥ 2K), drop them next
to the 2D drawings in the planning pack, and we have the dual-track
deliverable: planning-officer drawings (mine) + family / board-room renders
(yours from ChatGPT/Midjourney).
