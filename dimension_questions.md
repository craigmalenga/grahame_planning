## A. Conflicting or unclear dimensions

### A1. Rear-elevation horizontal sequence 51 + 60 + 49 + 123 + 17
- **Source:** `read_me.,md` and `Re_... 5.msg` (Grahame's note).
- 51 + 60 + 49 + 123 + 17 = **300 cm total**. 
- Job16873-1 (surveyor) shows the overall flat width as **4.34 m (434 cm)**.
- **Question:** Is the 51/60/49/123/17 sequence the *full* rear elevation, or only the section of rear wall belonging to the kitchen / upper-left of the L-shape? If only part, what is between the 17 cm "end of wall" and the start of the perpendicular face that contains the lower-basement double doors?

### A2. Sill heights "372 cm" and "380 cm" — datum unclear
- **Source:** `Re_... 5.msg` describes the existing kitchen window's height to sill as ~**372 cm**, and the rear windows' sill at ~**380 cm**.
- These are far too high to be measured from a kitchen floor. Almost certainly measured from the **lower-ground/courtyard floor** (i.e. the patio level below).
- **Question:** Confirm the datum point. If from courtyard floor, the staircase rise (~3.0 m) plus an additional ~70 cm sill = ~3.7 m, which matches roughly.

### A3. Internal opening between reception rooms — width not given
- **Source:** `Re_... 3.msg` — Grahame specifies *nibs* of 60 cm and 70 cm and a height of 290–300 cm, but **does not state the clear opening width**.
- **Question:** What is the final proposed clear opening width? Or should we calculate it from total wall length minus nibs minus internal wall thickness (10 cm)?

### A4. Side wall of rear reception room: 60 OR 70 cm?
- **Source:** `Re_... 3.msg` — "60 to 70 cm wall section" — Grahame is uncertain by 10 cm.
- **Question:** Site re-measure. The 60+70 nib split for the proposed opening may relate to this same wall.

### A5. Rear-reception window height 300–310 cm vs. ceiling 328 cm
- **Source:** re_msg3 says rear window is "300 cm to 310 cm high" with sill at 80 cm → head at 380–390 cm. Job16873 records ceiling at **328 cm**.
- **Conflict:** Window head can't be above ceiling. Either window height is overstated, or the 80 cm sill measurement is overstated, or there's a deep reveal / arched head condition.
- **Question:** Confirm head-of-window height above floor (probably ~290 cm, not 380 cm).

### A6. Kitchen window-to-become-door: "66 cm window gap, recess wider"
- **Source:** `Re_... 2.msg` — Grahame says the *actual window gap* is ~66 cm but the *structural recess* is wider.
- **Conflict with A1:** The rear-elevation sequence shows the existing larger window at **123 cm wide**. Is the 66 cm "actual gap" the GLASS pane within a 123 cm masonry recess, or is the 66 cm window a *different* small window than the 123 cm one?
- **Question:** Clarify whether (a) the 123 cm window in the rear elevation IS the same as the "small window" in the kitchen (i.e. only 66 cm of clear glass within a 123 cm reveal), or (b) the kitchen contains a *second smaller* window separate from the 123 cm one.

### A7. "300 cm rear room width" vs. "4.34 m flat width"
- **Source:** re_msg3 says rear reception is "roughly 300 cm across" on the relevant side. Job16873 surveyed flat width 434 cm.
- **Question:** Is the 300 cm an *internal* clear dimension within the room (i.e. between specific features such as wall returns / chimney breast)? Or is it the room's full internal width? If the latter, where do the other ~134 cm go?

### A8. Total rise vs. staircase length
- Staircase: 15 treads × 200–205 mm = 3.0–3.15 m rise; pole 3.3 m.
- Patio→threshold: per A2 datum analysis, ~3.0 m. Effectively matches but pole-to-landing geometry needs to be checked.
- **Question:** Final required rise from patio floor to the threshold of the new upper door — exact mm.

### A9. "290 cm to white line" inside basement recess
- **Source:** re_msg5 — height "to the white line above" is 290 cm in the lower-basement door recess.
- **Question:** Is this the lintel height of the basement-door opening, or a paint reveal line below the ceiling? Affects the head detail of the proposed pair of doors at lower-basement.
