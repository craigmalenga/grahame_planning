# Photoreal Brief — Compact Shower-Room — AFTER (proposed)

## SCENE
Interior view of a newly-built compact shower-room created within the existing ground-floor kitchen of a Kensington Victorian terrace. The room occupies a single-storey-height 2500 × 1500 mm strip, with four zones along the back wall left-to-right: shower, WC, basin, single front-loader washer. Entered via a pocket / sliding door on the kitchen side. Clean modern fit-out in a sympathetic restrained palette.

## LOCKED DIMENSIONS (do not invent or change)
- Room footprint: **2500 mm wide × 1500 mm deep × 2450 mm ceiling**
- Zones along back wall, left → right (with 100 mm partitions between):
  - **Shower 900 mm wide × 900 mm deep**, black-framed full-height glazed hinged door
  - **WC 500 mm zone**, wall-hung pan + concealed cistern, matt-black flush plate
  - **Basin 400 mm zone**, compact wall-hung white basin, matt-black tap
  - **Laundry 400 mm zone**, single front-loading 600-mm white washer (**NOT stacked**, no dryer above)
- Pocket / sliding door from the kitchen side: **700 mm clear opening**, full-height timber with black powder-coated track
- Small existing window (retained): ~900 × 1300 mm, sill at 720 mm (on the wall that gives onto the courtyard)
- Ceiling: 2450 mm, plain plaster

## MATERIALS
- Floor: **micro-cement, mid-grey**, with a falls-to-gully detail under the shower
- Shower zone walls (3 sides + ceiling-soffit): **large-format mid-grey porcelain tile**, full-height
- Non-shower walls: painted plaster, off-white
- Shower screen + door frame: **matt black powder-coated steel**
- Glazing: clear, low-iron
- WC, basin: white porcelain
- Brassware: **matt black**
- Pocket-door track + handle: matt black
- Washer: white
- Mirror over basin: full-width LED back-lit, frameless

## LIGHTING
- Soft daylight from above (skylight or roof-light, mid-morning)
- Recessed downlights in ceiling
- Discreet LED strip behind mirror (warm 2700 K)
- No harsh direct sun

## COMPOSITION
Camera at the pocket-door threshold (kitchen-side entry), eye-level ~1.65 m, looking along the 2500 mm run. Frame fills with the four-zone strip on the back wall in clear left-to-right order. Slight 3/4 angle so the shower glass + WC + basin + washer are all readable. 24 mm equivalent focal length.

## STYLE
Architectural / interiors photography, full-frame DSLR, 24 mm equivalent, f/8, ISO 400. RAW realism. No people. No CGI artefacts. Hotel-bathroom restraint with a single statement-piece — the all-black shower screen.

## REFERENCE
Aesthetic reference: contemporary London architectural shower-rooms (West One Bathrooms, CP Hart, Bert & May influence). Sympathetic to a listed-property context — no pop colours.

## ANTI-PATTERNS — do NOT produce
- Stacked washer + dryer above (single front-loader only — Rev B confirmed)
- Branded Merlyn shower hardware (out of scope for this flat — the Merlyn unit was for a different scheme)
- Pedestal basin — basin is **wall-hung**
- Floor-mounted WC — WC is **wall-hung**
- Decorative tile patterns — large-format plain porcelain
- Free-standing shower curtain — full-height fixed glass + hinged glass door only

## RECOMMENDED ITERATION VIEWS
1. **Hero** — from the pocket door looking along the 2500 mm strip (composition above).
2. **Reverse** — from the washer end looking back toward the pocket door.
3. **Detail of shower** — tight 50 mm on the black-framed glass door + hinge.
4. **Wide cutaway iso** — 3/4 isometric with ceiling removed, all four zones visible from above-side.
