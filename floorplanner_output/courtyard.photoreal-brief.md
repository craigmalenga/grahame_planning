## COMPOSITION

Frame: spiral staircase fills the right third; new glazed door visible on the kitchen-wall (upper-left); rear-access bay below it; rear-reception sash above. ## STYLE
Architectural photography, full-frame DSLR, 24 mm equivalent, f/8, ISO 200. RAW realism. No people. No CGI artefacts. Sympathetic to Grade-II listed Victorian aesthetic — restrained, not glossy.
