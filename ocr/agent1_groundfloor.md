## 2. Length logic vs the known envelope (GF 4.34 m × 13.77 m)

**Width (cross-wall):** The plan literally writes `4.34` across the front lounge = the surveyor's
4.34 m GF width. HIGH confidence the building is 4.34 m internally wide. Good anchor.

**Length (front-to-back), target = ~13.77 m overall:**
- Front lounge length = `570` (5.70 m).
- Rear zone long line = `670` (6.70 m).
- 5.70 + 6.70 = 12.40 m. Remaining to 13.77 m = ~1.37 m, which is consumed by:
  - the cross-wall / fireplace chimney-breast and the "new openings" wall thickness, and
  - the rear sanitary/back-door zone and rear external wall.
- This is internally CONSISTENT: 570 + 670 + intervening wall/rear-zone ≈ 13.77 m. PASS.

**Left-wall vertical chain (rear room), top→bottom:** 70 + 146 + 181 + 143 + 60 (cm)
= 70 + 146 + 181 + 143 + 60 = **600 cm = 6.00 m**.
This chain measures wall segments along the rear-room left (party) wall. 6.00 m sits just under
the 670 long line (670 includes the cross-wall/door return), so it is CONSISTENT. PASS.
(If a reviewer reads 181 as 18.1 or 60 as 6.0 the chain breaks — keep cm.)

**Fireplace-wall chain (front lounge left wall), top→bottom:** 198 + 180 + 192 (cm)
= **570 cm = 5.70 m**, which EXACTLY equals the `570` front-lounge length on the opposite side.
This is the strongest internal cross-check on the sheet: HIGH confidence.
198 (front-wall to fireplace) + 180 (fireplace width) + 192 (fireplace to cross-wall) = 570. PASS.

**Width cross-check from fittings:** shower-room wet zone `1.2m` + circulation + lounge must fit
inside 4.34 m. 1.2 m wet strip on one side leaves ~3.1 m of room width — plausible. PASS.
